## 今日句型

- **conditional**：If the company does not raise salaries, many experienced staff will leave within a year.
  - if 引导的真实条件句，主句用将来时。
- **negative**：Nobody in the course could reach the top of the hill before sunset.
  - 否定主语 nobody 后接肯定动词形式。
- **passive**：Every applicant is required to pay a small tax before the interview begins.
  - be required to do sth 表示“被要求做某事”。
- **wh**：What role do you think human emotions play in the decisions we make?
  - 特殊疑问句，do you think 作插入语，后面用陈述语序。
- **comparative**：The new drug works faster than the old one, but it costs twice as much.
  - 比较级 faster than 与倍数表达 twice as much 搭配。

## 阅读：A Small Town, A Big Change

When a **major** employer closes its doors, the whole town feels the **hit**. That was what happened in Marston three years ago, when the largest factory in the area announced that it would **remain** closed after the summer. For many families, the news meant months without a steady income, and the local council knew it had to act quickly.

The council decided to fund a training **course** for anyone who had lost a job. The idea was simple: give people new skills so that they could **reach** a better position in the job market. The **staff** at the community college agreed to teach evening classes, and the response surprised everyone.

One **major** reason for the programme's success was the **role** played by the former workers themselves. They persuaded friends and neighbours to join, and slowly the mood in the town began to move **towards** hope rather than anger. 'I had lost all **sense** of purpose,' one woman said. 'Now I feel I am part of something.'

The change did not happen overnight. Some residents still **require** help with rent and food bills, and the council has cut other services to pay for the scheme without raising the local **tax**. But the atmosphere on the high street is different now. People greet each other, and the empty shops are slowly filling up.

What the town learned is that a **society** is not simply a collection of individuals. It is a network of **human** connections, and when those connections are strong, a community can survive almost anything.
