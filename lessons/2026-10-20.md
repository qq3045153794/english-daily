## 今日句型

- **declarative**：Local volunteers provide free meals within the community centre.
  - 简单陈述句，主语+谓语+宾语+介词短语。
- **conditional**：If you follow the local law, you will avoid a lot of trouble.
  - if 引导条件状语从句，主句用将来时。
- **passive**：The new system was explained to all staff before it started.
  - 被动语态强调动作的承受者。
- **wh**：Which part of the state has the highest rainfall?
  - wh- 疑问句，which 作定语修饰 part。
- **comparative**：The local market is much cheaper than the supermarket in the city centre.
  - 比较级 cheaper than 用于比较两者。

## 阅读：A Library That Runs on Trust

In many **local** communities, the public library is more than a quiet place to read. It is a small **part** of the town's social **system**, and for some families it is the only free **area** where children can study after school.

The **fact** is that libraries **provide** far more than books. **within** a single building, a visitor can use the internet, join a language class, or ask for help with a job application. **however**, money is always a problem. When the **state** government cuts funding, the **local** council must decide how to spend what remains. Some residents argue **against** any reduction; others **point** out that taxes are already high.

Last year, our town tried something new. Volunteers formed a support group and followed a simple rule: every member gives two hours a month. The idea worked. The library stayed open, and the group now has real **power** in **local** politics. In **fact**, the council **might** change its **law** on public funding next year, because the volunteers showed that a community can protect what it values.

The **point** is simple. A library is not just a room full of books; it is a place where people learn to work together. If you **follow** the news in your **area**, you will probably find a similar story.

### 阅读理解

1. What can visitors do inside the library besides borrowing books?
2. What rule did the volunteer support group follow?
3. Why might the council change its law on public funding?
