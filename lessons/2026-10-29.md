## 今日句型

- **wh**：How would you describe the economic situation in your hometown?
  - how引导的特殊疑问句，用于询问看法。
- **conditional**：If you risk everything on one product, you may lose a lot.
  - if条件句＋情态动词，表示假设后果。
- **passive**：A special award was given to the best player of the season.
  - 被动语态，强调动作承受者。
- **relative**：The movie that we watched last night describes a family's rise to wealth.
  - that引导定语从句修饰movie。
- **comparative**：The rise in tuition fees is much faster than most families expected.
  - much＋比较级，表示程度差异。

## 阅读：A Small Phone, a Big Lesson

Last week our teacher asked us to **describe** a **product** that had changed our daily life. Most students chose the **phone**, and the discussion soon turned into a debate about its costs and benefits.

One **player** in the debate was Lin, who had bought a **used** **phone** with her own money. She said the **rise** in screen time among teenagers worried her, and she kept a **record** of how many hours she spent online each day. Her figures were surprising: almost four hours on weekdays.

Our teacher then asked a **certain** question that made the room quiet: what do we really gain from all this time? A **special** guest, a young engineer, joined us by video. She explained that every **economic** decision in her company starts with one simple test — does this **product** help people learn, or only keep them amused?

She also described a **movie** about a village school where **education** mattered more than money. The film won an **award**, she said, not because it was beautiful, but because it was honest.

At the end, we all agreed on one point. Technology is not a **risk** in itself; the real **loss** comes when we stop asking questions about it.

### 阅读理解

1. What did the teacher ask the students to describe?
2. Why did Lin keep a record of her screen time?
3. According to the engineer, what simple test does every product face?
