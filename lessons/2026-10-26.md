## 今日句型

- **yes_no**：Is it likely that the new rules will affect small businesses in the community?
  - “It is likely that...”用形式主语表达可能性，是四六级写作高频句型。
- **wh**：Why did the local authority refuse to discuss the data with the community?
  - why 引导特殊疑问句，常用于追问原因，多见于阅读与听力题。
- **passive**：The trade agreement was signed by both nations last Friday.
  - 被动语态把重点放在“协议”上，说明动作承受者。
- **conditional**：If the union accepts the offer, the strike will probably end this week.
  - 真实条件句：主将从现，从句用一般现在时。
- **comparative**：The new site is more convenient for the community than the old one.
  - 比较级 + than，注意用 one 代替前面出现过的名词。

## 阅读：A Community Project That Changed a Nation's Trade

When a small coastal **community** decided to rebuild its harbour, nobody expected the project to attract national attention. The work began at an early **stage** with a simple survey. Volunteers collected **data** on tides, fishing catches and the number of boats using the port. The **range** of information was wider than anyone had imagined.

The local **authority** was at first unwilling to help. Officials said the **site** was too expensive to develop and that the rules on coastal building were strict. However, the fishing **union** argued that the harbour was the **cause** of most jobs in the area, and that losing it would be a disaster for every family.

After months of discussion, the two sides agreed to hold a public **event** where residents could ask questions. It was **probably** the most crowded meeting the town had ever seen. Speakers explained that the new harbour was **likely** to increase **trade** with nearby ports and would create work for young people.

The turning point came when the **nation**'s transport ministry offered support. Engineers studied the plans and confirmed that the chosen **site** was safe. The **stage** was now set for building to begin.

Two years later, the harbour is open, and the **authority** now uses the project as an example for other towns. The lesson is simple: good **data** and **patient** discussion can change decisions.

1. What did the volunteers collect information about?

3. What finally made the project possible?
