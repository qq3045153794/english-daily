## 今日句型

- **wh**：What sort of music do you usually listen to while you are studying?
  - what sort of 用于询问种类，后面直接跟名词。
- **passive**：The final report was produced by a team of four students.
  - 被动语态强调动作的承受者，by 引出执行者。
- **conditional**：If the police act quickly, the situation can still be controlled.
  - 真实条件句：if 从句用一般现在时，主句用情态动词加动词原形。
- **comparative**：The value of the house is much higher than it was two years ago.
  - 比较级常与 than 连用，much 用来加强语气。
- **relative**：The square, which lies in the centre of the town, is now full of children.
  - 非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，补充说明先行词。

## 阅读：The Square That the Town Rebuilt

For years, the square in the **centre** of our town was a place people simply walked through. Then, **upon** a cold morning in 2019, a group of neighbours decided to change it. Their aim was to **create** something of real **value** for the **public** — a small park where children could play and old friends could talk.

The project was not easy. For a **period** of almost two years, the council argued about money. The final **figure** they agreed on was modest, and there was little official **force** behind the plan. In **general**, people expected the idea to fail. But the neighbours kept working. They learned to **sort** rubbish, paint benches and plant trees, and an old stone **god** near the gate was cleaned until it shone again.

One Saturday the **police** closed the road so that children could paint a long blue river on the ground. That single **action** surprised everybody. A local carpenter produced fifty wooden chairs for free, and a teacher began a weekly reading hour under the trees.

Nothing killed the project — not rain, not criticism, not even a broken water pipe in the winter. Today the square is full of noise and colour, and visitors often ask how it all began. The answer is simple: a few people took **action**, and the whole town followed.
