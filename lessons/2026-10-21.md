## 今日句型

- **conditional**：If you practise every day, you will not lose your confidence before the test.
  - if 引导的真实条件句，主句用 will + 动词原形。
- **passive**：A new policy was added to the school rules last term.
  - 被动语态强调动作的承受者，by 短语常可省略。
- **relative**：The guy who won the first prize is my cousin.
  - who 引导定语从句，修饰表示人的先行词。
- **wh**：What is the real reason for the rise in the crime rate?
  - 特殊疑问句，问原因常用 What is the reason for...?
- **comparative**：Although the court is small, it is busier than any other in the city.
  - although 引导让步状语从句，不能与 but 连用。

## 阅读：The Art of Teamwork

Last **term**, our school allowed students to join a new **art** programme. The idea came from a **guy** in my class, who said that the **real** problem was not talent but time. **Although** many students wanted to take part, few could stay **later** than four o'clock.

In my **view**, the answer was simple: we needed a **team**. **Later** that month, five of us formed one. We agreed to **add** two hours of practice each week and to keep a record of our progress. The **rate** of improvement was slow at first, and we almost had to **lose** hope. But the **policy** of sharing work helped: whoever painted best explained his method to the others.

By the end of the **term**, we had to show our work in a competition. The judges did not **allow** us to change anything after the first round. Our **team** did not **win** the top prize, but we won the audience vote. A teacher told us that the **court** of public opinion mattered more than any score.

In the long **term**, I think we learned something more useful than a prize. Nobody can **win** every time, but a group that trusts each other will never **lose** its direction.

### 阅读理解

1. Why was time a problem for many students who wanted to join the programme?
2. What did the five students agree to do every week?
3. How did the team do in the competition?
