## 今日句型

- **yes_no**：Did you take part in the discussion about the new government program?
  - 一般疑问句，take part in 表示“参加”。
- **wh**：Why does this issue happen again and again in rural areas?
  - why 引导特殊疑问句，happen 为不及物动词。
- **passive**：The social program was designed to help kids from poor families.
  - 被动语态 be + 过去分词，强调动作承受者。
- **conditional**：If the government raises the level of support, more families might stay.
  - if 引导真实条件句，might 表示可能性。
- **comparative**：The war caused far more damage than the report states.
  - more...than 比较结构，states 作动词“陈述”。

## 阅读：A Town That Learned to Read

When a small town in the north lost its main factory in 2019, few people expected anything good to **happen**. **However**, a group of parents decided to act. They asked the local **government** for help and started a free reading **program** for kids in the **area**.

The idea was simple. Every Saturday morning, children meet in the library and read aloud to volunteers. The **social** side matters as much as the books: shy children slowly learn to speak in front of others. "Reading is only **part** of it," says one teacher. "They also learn to listen."

The results have been encouraging. Test scores in the town have risen to a **level** above the national average. Parents **state** that their children now ask for books instead of games. There is still a long **line** of families waiting to join.

Money remains an **issue**, **however**. The **program** depends on donations, and the building is old. If the town council cuts funding next year, the reading club **might** close. Some residents argue that the **government** should pay for such projects everywhere, not only in rich cities.

What began as a small answer to a local problem now leads to a wider question: who should support education outside school? The town has no **war** to fight, but it is fighting something quieter — the belief that nothing can change.

### 阅读理解

1. Why did the parents start the reading program?
2. According to the teacher, what else do the children learn besides reading?
3. What could cause the reading club to close?

<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

1. Because the town had lost its main factory and they wanted to help local children.
2. They learn to listen and to speak in front of other people.
3. A lack of money, especially if the town council cuts its funding.
