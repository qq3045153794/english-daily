## 今日句型

- **declarative**：The council offered local businesses a short training course.
  - offer sb sth 双宾语结构，表示“向某人提供某物”。
- **yes_no**：Did the new rules have any effect on small businesses?
  - 一般疑问句用助动词 did，动词还原为原形。
- **wh**：What type of support interests young people most?
  - wh-问句，主语 type of support 为单数，动词加 -s。
- **passive**：At least ten new jobs were created as a result.
  - 被动语态强调结果本身，by 短语常省略。
- **conditional**：If the council acts now, the effect will be much smaller.
  - 条件句遵循“主将从现”原则。

## 阅读：A Small Town Decides to Act

When a small town loses its **main** employer, the **effect** on local families can be serious. Last spring, the **council** in Millbrook decided to **act** before the **result** became worse. They asked local **business** owners what kind of help they needed, and they offered free training in digital skills.

The **offer** was small at first. **Perhaps** twenty people signed up, and at **least** half of them were over fifty. However, the numbers grew quickly. Within six months, the results appeared to surprise everyone: three new shops opened, and two existing firms began to sell online.

The lesson is simple. Support does not have to cost a lot to produce a real **effect**. The **council** spent **less** than ten thousand pounds, yet the **type** of help it offered matched what people actually needed. The training included advice on **national** tax rules and on how to write a short **business** plan.

Not everyone showed **interest** at the beginning. Some owners said they had no time; others did not believe that a town **council** could help a **business**. The organisers listened, changed the timetable, and added evening classes for people who worked during the day.

Today Millbrook is not rich, but it is more confident. One small decision — to **act** early rather than wait — has changed the mood of the whole town, and other councils have asked to copy the plan.

### 阅读理解

1. Why did the council in Millbrook decide to act?
2. What kind of help did the council offer at first?
3. What does the writer suggest is the main lesson of the story?

2. Free training in digital skills, with evening classes added later.
