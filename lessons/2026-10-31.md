## 今日句型

- **conditional**：If you list the tasks in detail, the project will be easier to manage.
  - 条件句：主句常用一般将来时。
- **passive**：A large amount of evidence was collected by the research team.
  - 被动语态强调动作的承受者。
- **relative**：The method, which saved us two hours, was suggested by a recent graduate.
  - 非限制性定语从句补充说明，用逗号隔开。
- **wh**：Why did the scheme reduce costs so quickly?
  - 特殊疑问句：疑问词提前，助动词紧跟主语。
- **negative**：The results did not occur as we had expected.
  - 一般过去时的否定用 did not + 动词原形。

## 阅读：A Small Scheme, A Big Change

In a **recent** meeting, our town council approved a small **scheme** to **reduce** plastic waste in local shops. The **project** began with a simple **method**: volunteers would **list** every plastic item sold in ten participating stores and record the **amount** of packaging for each one.

The first results were surprising. Nearly forty per cent of the waste came from just five products, and this pattern did not **occur** by chance. Shop owners were asked to give a full **account** of their supply chains, and the **detail** they provided showed where cheaper alternatives already existed. Under these conditions, most stores agreed to change suppliers within three months.

Critics argued that the survey offered little **evidence** of real change and that a voluntary **scheme** would never **save** money. To answer them, the council repeated the count in June and published the figures by **sex**, age and income, so that every household could judge the effect on its own budget.

The numbers are now clear: plastic use has fallen by 28 per cent, and the town has saved about 90,000 yuan in a single year. The lesson is simple. A careful **method** matters more than a loud promise.

### 阅读理解

1. What did the volunteers have to record in each of the ten stores?
2. Why did the council publish the figures by sex, age and income?
3. By how much has plastic use fallen since the project began?

1. Every plastic item sold and the amount of packaging.
2. So that every household could judge the effect on its own budget.
3. By 28 per cent.
