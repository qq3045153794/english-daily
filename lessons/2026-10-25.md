## 今日句型

- **declarative**：The whole team agreed to continue the survey until the end of the year.
  - whole 强调“整个的”，continue 后可直接跟名词或不定式。
- **passive**：The novel is based on the history of a small industrial town.
  - be based on 表示“以……为基础/根据”。
- **conditional**：If you consider the whole process, the result seems quite reasonable.
  - if 条件句中用一般现在时，主句常用一般现在时或将来时。
- **negative**：The young minister could not control his feelings during the ceremony.
  - could not + 动词原形，表示过去未能做到某事。
- **wh**：Why did the club decide to move toward a quieter part of town?
  - 特殊疑问句用助动词 did，其后动词用原形；toward 表方向。

## 阅读：The Town That Said No to Oil

For most of its **history**, the small fishing town of Ardmore was known for two things: its **church** and its **club** for retired sailors. Life moved slowly there, and the **whole** town seemed to share a single **voice**. Then, in 2019, a company arrived to look for **oil** under the sea. The **process** of obtaining a permit took months, and the **political** debate that followed divided neighbours.

The town council decided to **consider** the plan carefully. Some residents wanted the money; others feared for the fish. A local **minister**, who had lived in Ardmore for forty years, spoke at a public meeting. "We should not let outsiders **control** our future," he said, and **thus** the argument began.

Instead of fighting, the town chose to act together. People walked **toward** the harbour every Sunday with signs, and students started a science **club** to study the sea. Their **voice** was quiet but steady, and it was based on facts rather than anger. Slowly, public opinion turned.

In the end, the company agreed to **continue** talks and to reduce the size of the project. Ardmore did not win everything, but it gained something more valuable: **control** over its own story. Visitors often ask how a town of nine hundred people changed the mind of a large company. The answer is simple: they kept speaking, and they spoke as one.
