今日句型

  • declarative：The former president of the company now leads a small research unit.
  • 简单陈述句，former 作前置定语修饰 president。
  • comparative：This model is rather more accurate than the one we tested last year.
  • rather 修饰比较级，表示“相当、更……”。
  • conditional：If you apply for the position early, you will have a better chance.
  • if 引导条件状语从句，主句用将来时。
  • passive：The new model was tested three times before it was sold.
  • 被动语态 was tested，强调动作承受者。
  • wh：How much space do we actually need for the experiment?
  • wh 疑问句，actually 表示“实际上”，常放动词前。

阅读：A Late Start, a Better Ending

In 2019, the former head of a small research unit at a northern university made a decision that surprised everyone. Rather than retire quietly, he chose to seek a new position at a national space agency, where he could apply thirty years of experience to a project he had long dreamed of.

The team's task was simple to describe but hard to do. They hoped to build a computer model of how dust moves through space, a problem that had puzzled engineers for nearly a century. Every step of the work required patience. The first version failed its test badly, and the team had to return to the beginning.

Actually, the failure helped. It showed that their ideas worked only in a narrow range of conditions, and the group needed more basic research before going further. The new leader asked for extra room in the budget and time to check each assumption.

Two years of steady effort followed. When the final results appeared, the agency's president called the model "the most useful tool we have." The position he had taken at sixty-five had turned into the most productive period of his career.

阅读理解

  1. What did the former head of the research unit decide to do instead of retiring?
  2. Why did the team's first failure turn out to be useful?
  3. How long did the team's steady effort last before the final results appeared?
参考答案 1. He chose to seek a new position at a national space agency. 2. It showed that their ideas worked only in a narrow range of conditions. 3. About two years.
