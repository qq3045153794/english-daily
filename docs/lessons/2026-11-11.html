今日单词

1. fund /fʌnd/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：基金，资金；提供资金
  • 搭配：raise funds for
  • 例句：The charity hopes to raise funds for a new library in the village.
  • 翻译：这家慈善机构希望为村里的一座新图书馆筹集资金。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

2. central /'sentrәl/ adj. 🔊

  • 中文：中央的；核心的，重要的
  • 搭配：play a central role in
  • 例句：Tourism plays a central role in the economy of this small island.
  • 翻译：旅游业在这个小岛的经济中起着核心作用。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

3. therefore /'ðєәfɒ:/ adv. 🔊

  • 中文：因此，所以
  • 搭配：and therefore
  • 例句：The road was closed, and therefore we had to take a longer route.
  • 翻译：那条路封了，因此我们不得不走一条更长的路线。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

4. sign /sain/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：标志，招牌，迹象；签名
  • 搭配：a clear sign of
  • 例句：There is no sign that the weather will improve before Friday.
  • 翻译：没有任何迹象表明周五之前天气会好转。
  • 句型：negative
  • 类型：新词

5. prove /pru:v/ v. 🔊

  • 中文：证明，证实；证明是
  • 搭配：prove to be
  • 例句：It has been proved that regular exercise improves memory and mood.
  • 翻译：已经证明，经常锻炼能改善记忆力和情绪。
  • 句型：passive
  • 类型：新词

6. choose /tʃu:z/ v. 🔊

  • 中文：选择，挑选
  • 搭配：choose between
  • 例句：If you choose the cheaper ticket, you will have to change trains twice.
  • 翻译：如果你选便宜的那张票，你就得换两次火车。
  • 句型：conditional
  • 类型：新词

7. argue /'ɑ:gju/ v. 🔊

  • 中文：争论，辩论；主张
  • 搭配：argue that
  • 例句：Some economists argue that higher taxes will slow down the economy.
  • 翻译：一些经济学家主张，提高税收会拖慢经济发展。

  • 类型：新词

8. theory /'θiәri/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：理论，学说；推测
  • 搭配：in theory
  • 例句：The theory that he proposed in 1998 is still widely discussed today.

  • 类型：新词

  • 搭配：a source of

今日句型

  • conditional：If the government provides more funds, the school can buy new computers.
  • if 引导条件状语从句，主句用情态动词表结果。
  • passive：The theory was proved correct by later experiments.
  • 被动语态 be + 过去分词，强调动作承受者。
  • relative：The lady who signed the contract is the manager of a royal hotel.
  • who 引导定语从句修饰 the lady。
  • wh：Why did you choose this book as your main source of information?
  • 特殊疑问句：疑问词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词。
  • comparative：The new bridge is three miles shorter, and therefore the trip takes less time.
  • 比较级 + therefore 表示因果与对比。

阅读：The Long Road to Clean Water

Every morning, Maria walks nearly a mile from her village to a small well. For years, this well was the only source of clean water for three hundred families, and the central problem was simple: there was not enough of it. Therefore, local leaders decided to fight for a better system.

Last spring, a royal charity agreed to fund a new water project. Engineers came to test the soil and prove that a deeper well could serve the whole valley. The theory was that underground water flowed along an old river bed, and the early results seemed to support it. Villagers had to choose where the new well should stand, and they argued for weeks about the best spot. Some experts argue that such decisions should be made by engineers alone, but the village lady who led the meetings disagreed. For her, the quality of the discussion mattered as much as the final choice. When the agreement was finally signed, everyone clapped.

Today, a sign beside the well says: “Water is life.” Maria no longer waits in long lines. Her daughter, who studies cells in a biology class at school, tells her that water is inside every one of them. Maria smiles: for her, that is not just science but a daily truth.

阅读理解

  1. What was the central problem in Maria's village?
  2. How did the villagers decide where to put the new well?
  3. What does the sign beside the well say?
参考答案 1. There was not enough clean water for the families. 2. They argued for weeks and then chose the best spot together. 3. It says “Water is life.”
