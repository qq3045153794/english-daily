今日单词

1. part /pɑ:t/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 部分；角色；零件 v. 分开
  • 搭配：play a part in
  • 例句：Parents play an important part in shaping their children's daily habits.
  • 翻译：父母在塑造孩子日常习惯方面起着重要作用。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：错词

2. area /'єәriә/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 区域；面积；领域
  • 搭配：a rural area
  • 例句：Is this area safe for children to play in after dark?
  • 翻译：这个区域天黑后孩子在里面玩安全吗？
  • 句型：yes_no
  • 类型：错词

3. however /hau'evә/ adv. / conj. 🔊

  • 中文：adv. 然而；无论如何
  • 搭配：however hard
  • 例句：If it rains tomorrow, however, the match will be moved indoors.
  • 翻译：不过，如果明天下雨，比赛将改在室内进行。
  • 句型：conditional
  • 类型：错词

4. state /steit/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 状态；州 v. 陈述，说明
  • 搭配：in a poor state
  • 例句：The safety rules are clearly stated on the first page of the form.
  • 翻译：安全规则清楚地写在了表格的第一页上。
  • 句型：passive
  • 类型：复习

5. might /mait/ aux. / n. 🔊

  • 中文：aux. 可能，也许 n. 力量
  • 搭配：might well
  • 例句：She might not agree with our decision, but she will certainly listen.
  • 翻译：她可能不同意我们的决定，但她肯定会听。
  • 句型：negative
  • 类型：复习

6. social /'sәuʃәl/ adj. 🔊

  • 中文：adj. 社会的；社交的
  • 搭配：social media
  • 例句：People who spend hours on social media often say they feel tired.
  • 翻译：花数小时在社交媒体上的人常说他们感到疲惫。
  • 句型：relative
  • 类型：新词

7. government /'gʌvәnmәnt/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 政府
  • 搭配：the local government
  • 例句：The government has promised to build more schools in mountain villages.
  • 翻译：政府已承诺在山区村庄建更多学校。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

8. lead /li:d. led/ v. / n. 🔊

  • 中文：v. 领导；导致；通向 n. 领先
  • 搭配：lead to
  • 例句：What led you to choose this subject at university?
  • 翻译：是什么让你在大学选择了这个专业？
  • 句型：wh
  • 类型：新词

9. war /wɒ:/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 战争；斗争
  • 搭配：go to war
  • 例句：The war forced thousands of families to leave their homes.
  • 翻译：那场战争迫使数千家庭离开家园。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

10. level /'levl/ n. / adj. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 水平；级别；高度 adj. 平的
  • 搭配：sea level
  • 例句：Her English level is now much higher than it was last year.
  • 翻译：她的英语水平现在比去年高多了。
  • 句型：comparative
  • 类型：新词

11. kid /kid/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 小孩 v. 开玩笑，哄骗
  • 搭配：a bright kid
  • 例句：Every kid in the class wanted to try the new game.
  • 翻译：班上每个孩子都想试试这个新游戏。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

12. line /lain/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 线；队列；线路 v. 排队
  • 搭配：stand in line
  • 例句：We waited in line for over an hour to buy the tickets.
  • 翻译：我们排队等了一个多小时才买到票。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

13. happen /'hæpәn/ v. 🔊

  • 中文：v. 发生；碰巧
  • 搭配：happen to
  • 例句：What happened to your bike while you were at school?
  • 翻译：你在学校的时候你的自行车怎么了？
  • 句型：wh
  • 类型：新词

14. issue /'isju/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 问题；议题 v. 发布，发行
  • 搭配：a serious issue
  • 例句：Climate change is one of the most serious issues we face today.
  • 翻译：气候变化是我们今天面临的最严重的问题之一。
  • 句型：comparative
  • 类型：新词

15. program /'prәugræm/ n. / v. 🔊

  • 中文：n. 节目；计划；程序 v. 规划
  • 搭配：a training program
  • 例句：The new training program was designed for first-year students.
  • 翻译：这个新的培训项目是为一年级学生设计的。
  • 句型：passive
  • 类型：新词

今日句型

  • conditional：If the government invests more in public transport, traffic in the city centre will improve.
  • if 引导的真实条件句，主句用将来时。
  • passive：The new program was announced by the local government last week.
  • 被动语态强调动作的承受者。
  • relative：Students who take part in social activities often speak with more confidence.
  • who 引导定语从句，修饰 students。
  • wh：What actually happened at the meeting on Friday evening?
  • what 作主语的特殊疑问句。
  • comparative：The noise level in this area is much higher than it was five years ago.
  • much 修饰比较级，表示程度更大。

阅读：A Small Town Takes On a Big Job

Every kid in Millbrook knows the old railway line at the edge of town. For years it was a place where nothing much happened. Last spring, however, a group of parents asked the local government for help. The area beside the tracks had become dirty and unsafe, and they wanted it turned into a park.

The government agreed to support a small program. Volunteers would clean the ground, plant trees and build a short walking path. The parents hoped the project would lead to something bigger: a safe place where children could play after school.

Not everyone was sure it would work. One official spoke about the poor state of the town's budget and warned that the work might take more than a year. But the volunteers refused to give up. They worked on weekends, and slowly the land began to change.

By autumn, the park was open. It is not a grand place, but it has a basketball court, a few benches and a garden. Local teachers say it has already improved social life in the town, because families now meet there and talk.

For the children of Millbrook, the lesson is simple. Big problems do not always need a war or a huge budget; sometimes a small group of people at the level of an ordinary street can make a real difference.

阅读理解

  1. Why did the parents ask the local government for help?
  2. What did the official warn about?
  3. How has the park changed social life in the town?
参考答案 1. Because the area beside the railway line was dirty and unsafe. 2. That the work might take more than a year because the budget was poor. 3. Families now meet there and talk, so people know each other better.
