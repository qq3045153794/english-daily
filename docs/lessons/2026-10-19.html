今日单词

1. part /pɑ:t/ n./v. 🔊

  • 中文：部分；角色；分开
  • 搭配：play a part in
  • 例句：Reading English newspapers is an important part of my daily study routine.
  • 翻译：读英文报纸是我日常学习中很重要的一部分。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

  • 搭配：a rural area
  • 例句：Which area of the city has the best public transport?
  • 翻译：这座城市的哪个区域公共交通最方便？

  • 类型：新词

今日句型

  • conditional：If the local government provides better buses, fewer people might drive to work.
  • if 引导条件句，主句用 might 表示可能性。
  • passive：The new system was explained to all the staff within one week.
  • 被动语态 was explained，强调动作承受者。
  • wh：Which part of the law is against small businesses?
  • which 引导特殊疑问句，问具体部分。
  • negative：He did not follow the advice, and in fact he regretted it later.
  • 否定句常用 did not + 动词原形。
  • relative：The report, which states the main facts, will be sent to the local office.
  • 非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，which 指代 report。

阅读：Who Decides? A Small Town Tests Its Local Power

In many countries, the state gives local governments the power to make decisions about small things. however, the fact that a town can pass its own rules does not mean that everyone will agree.

Last spring, officials in a small town in the northern part of the country proposed a new law about public spaces. The rule would provide free bicycles in the central shopping area. Supporters said the system would help students and workers travel cheaply. They pointed to cities where similar plans had worked well.

Not everyone was convinced. Some shop owners spoke against the plan. Their main point was simple: the streets are already crowded, and more bicycles might cause accidents. One resident said the town should follow the example of a neighbouring city, where the plan failed within a year.

The debate shows how local politics works. A council cannot simply announce a rule and expect people to accept it. It must explain the facts, listen to criticism and change part of the plan if necessary. In the end, the town agreed to test the bicycles for six months. If the results are good, the state will provide extra money. If not, the plan will end. Either way, the decision stays within the community.

阅读理解

  1. What did the town propose last spring?
  2. Why did some shop owners speak against the plan?
  3. What did the town finally decide to do?
参考答案 1. A new law about public spaces, including free bicycles in the shopping area. 2. Because the streets are already crowded and more bicycles might cause accidents. 3. To test the bicycle plan for six months.
