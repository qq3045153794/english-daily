今日单词

1. activity /æk'tiviti/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：活动；活跃
  • 搭配：outdoor activity
  • 例句：Reading is a quiet activity that many students enjoy after a long day.
  • 翻译：阅读是一种安静的活动，很多学生在漫长的一天后喜欢做这件事。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

今日句型

  • declarative：A couple of students finally finished the group activity at midnight.
  • a couple of 表示“几个”，finally 常与完成动作连用。
  • passive：The safety rules were explained to everyone before the race started.
  • 被动语态 explain sth to sb 变为 sth be explained to sb。
  • conditional：If you set a clear goal, you will find it easier to keep going.
  • 真实条件句，主句用 will + 动词原形。
  • negative：He did not drop out of the course, because his family supported him.
  • drop out of 表示“退出”，否定用助动词 did not。
  • wh：Why do so many people share their daily activities on social media?
  • why 引导特殊疑问句，share sth on social media 为常用搭配。

阅读：A Town Race Worth Running

Every autumn, the small town of Willow Creek holds a common tradition that almost everyone joins: a ten-kilometre race along the river. This year, a couple from our street, Anna and Peter, finally agreed to take part. Their main goal was simple — to finish together, not to win.

The local board of the sports club helped to lay out the route and to explain the safety rules to every runner. Neighbours came to support them, handing out water and cheering from the bridges. Anna said the activity was indeed harder than she had expected; the last hill made her pace drop sharply. But Peter stayed beside her, and they shared the last bottle of water.

What I like about this event is its nature: it is not really about speed. People of every age and background run side by side, and the federal sports fund pays for the timing chips, so nobody has to pay a fee. After the race, families sit on the grass, share food and talk about next year.

Anna crossed the line in ninety minutes, smiling through her tiredness. Indeed, she told me afterwards, the race gave her something a gym never could: the feeling that a whole town was running with her.

阅读理解

  1. What was Anna and Peter's main goal in the race?
  2. Who paid for the timing chips?
  3. What made Anna's pace drop sharply?
参考答案 1. To finish the race together, not to win. 2. The federal sports fund. 3. The last hill on the route.
