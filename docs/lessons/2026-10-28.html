今日单词

1. section /'sekʃәn/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：部分；区域；部门
  • 搭配：a section of the report
  • 例句：This section of the report explains how the company handles customer complaints.
  • 翻译：报告的这一部分说明了公司如何处理顾客投诉。
  • 句型：declarative
  • 类型：新词

2. leader /'li:dә/ n. 🔊

  • 中文：领导者；领袖
  • 搭配：team leader
  • 例句：The leader who spoke first was calm, clear and surprisingly honest.
  • 翻译：第一个发言的领导者沉着、清晰，而且出人意料地坦诚。
  • 句型：relative
  • 类型：新词

3. deal /di:l/ v./n. 🔊

  • 中文：处理；交易；协议
  • 搭配：deal with
  • 例句：How do you usually deal with complaints from customers?
  • 翻译：你通常如何处理顾客的投诉？
  • 句型：wh
  • 类型：新词

  • 句型：declarative

今日句型

  • passive：A large section of the old factory was destroyed by fire.
  • 被动语态强调动作的承受者，常用于新闻和报告。
  • conditional：If the leader accepts the deal, the whole industry will change.
  • 条件句：if 从句用一般现在时，主句用将来时。
  • wh：Why did the firm claim that the decision involved no new costs?
  • 特殊疑问句常用于阅读理解提问与讨论。
  • relative：The company that made the top image is based in London.
  • 关系从句用 that/which 修饰前面的名词。
  • negative：We could not accept the offer because no tickets were available.
  • 否定句加原因状语，是写作中常见的解释结构。

阅读：A Small Cinema's Big Decision

When the old King's Cinema in Millford closed in 2019, few people believed it would ever open again. The building had stood at the top of the High Street for over eighty years, and for many local people it was part of the town's image.

Last month, however, a small local firm announced that it had bought the site. The company's leader, Anna Reed, claimed that the new cinema would show films from every section of the industry, not just big international productions. 'We want a place where students, families and older people can all find something they enjoy,' she said.

The project will involve more than forty volunteers, who will repair the seats and paint the walls. Tickets will be available online and at the door, and the cheapest seat will cost only six pounds.

Not everyone is happy. Some residents worry about traffic, and a few shop owners say they were never asked for their opinion. The town council must still accept the final plans, and a decision is expected in November.

Anna is confident. She says she has already made a deal with two film clubs in the city, and that the first screening will take place before Christmas. 'This town deserves its cinema back,' she added. 'The Lord Mayor himself has promised to come.'

阅读理解

  1. Where is the old cinema located?
  2. How much will the cheapest ticket cost?
  3. When is the council's decision expected?
参考答案 1. At the top of the High Street in Millford. 2. Six pounds. 3. In November.
