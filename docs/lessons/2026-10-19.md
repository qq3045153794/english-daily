## 今日句型

- **wh**：Which part of the state has the highest unemployment rate?
  - wh-问句用于询问具体信息，注意语序为疑问词+助动词+主语。
- **passive**：The local system was improved within two months.
  - 被动语态强调动作的承受者，结构为 be + 过去分词。
- **conditional**：If the government provides more power, the factories might reopen.
  - 条件句：if 从句用一般现在时，主句用情态动词+动词原形。
- **relative**：The law, which was passed last year, still has many critics.
  - 非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，补充说明先行词。
- **negative**：He did not follow the local rules against smoking.
  - 否定句借助助动词 do/does/did + not 构成。

## 阅读：Power for a Small Town

In the northern **part** of our **state**, a small town has changed the way it gets its **power**. For many years, the **local** council bought electricity from one large company. The **fact** was simple: prices rose every winter, and some families struggled to pay. **however**, nobody wanted to act **against** the energy company without a better plan.

Last spring, residents voted to build a small solar **system** on empty land beside the river. The **area** is not large, but it receives plenty of sunlight. **within** twelve months, the panels were providing clean electricity to more than three hundred homes. The council also passed a new **law** so that the project followed national safety rules.

Supporters **point** out that the scheme has done more than cut bills. It has created jobs and brought neighbours together. Critics argue that solar **power** **might** fail on cloudy days, and on that **point** they are right. The town still needs the main grid for backup.

The lesson is clear. Small projects can work when people trust each other. Any community can take **part** in shaping its own future if it plans carefully and provides honest information to everyone.

### 阅读理解

1. Why did the town want to change the way it got its power?
2. What did the council do to make sure the project was safe?
3. What do critics say about solar power?
